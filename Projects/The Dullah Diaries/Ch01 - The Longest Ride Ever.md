## Chapter 1: The Longest Ride Ever

***Monday, November 3rd***

***Just got off the world's most ancient taxi after what felt like a million hours. It was only supposed to be seven, but trust me, my butt says it was way longer.***

[Illustration suggestion: Abdullah in the back of a cramped taxi, squished between a chicken cage and a woman holding the cage, looking miserable.]

***Finally, we stop in front of this shop that's supposed to be my "welcome to your new life" spot or something. The sign says "Vonnie's," but it looks like it survived a couple of wars itself.***

[Illustration suggestion: A ramshackle shop with a skewed sign reading "Vonnie's," and Abdullah standing outside with his giant suitcase, looking confused.]

***Did I mention I've been orphaned? Yeah. Thanks to some stupid war back home in Congo. Mom and Dad were heroes, fighting for the good guys, and then—bam!—they're gone. And here I am, dumped in South Africa with some uncle I've never even heard of.***

***Been sitting on this rock for ages, watching people go in and out of the shop. I started throwing pebbles at my big toe because, you know, boredom. Then, out of nowhere, the sun decides to bail on me. I look up, and there's this super tall shadow looming over me.***

[Illustration suggestion: Abdullah sitting on a rock, tossing pebbles at his toe, with a long shadow suddenly appearing over him.]

***The shadows all like, "You, Lizzie’s boy? You Dullah?" His voice sounds like he's been gargling gravel.***

***It takes me a second to realize he's talking about my mom, Elizabeth. So I tell him I'm Abdullah, but he's already telling me to hurry up because the goats won't wait. Like I care about goats right now.***

[Illustration suggestion: Abdullah, squinting up at a tall, shadowy figure, with a thought bubble that says, "Who's Lizzie?"]

***Turns out the shadow guy is actually pretty short. It's Uncle Jan, who seems like he's been wrestling with life since he was born. And those eyes—man, if looks could kill, I'd be pushing up daisies right next to that sign.***

***He tells me I'm Dullah now, he doesn’t have time for long names, which I guess is his way of saying "Welcome to the family." Then he points to this donkey cart that's supposed to take us home. It's like something out of an old movie where everything goes wrong.***

[Illustration suggestion: A short, tough-looking Uncle Jan with steely eyes, standing next to a donkey cart, with Abdullah looking down at him, taken aback.]

***So, here I am, sitting on a cart that's bumping along like it's got a grudge against me, heading to a place I've never seen before. And all I can think is: what kind of weird adventure have I gotten myself into?***

[Illustration suggestion: Abdullah on a rickety cart, holding onto his suitcase for dear life as the donkeys pull him along a dusty road.]
